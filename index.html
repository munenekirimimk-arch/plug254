Questions owners ask

Does it work with my existing WhatsApp number?+

Yes. Pally runs on the WhatsApp Business Platform. You keep your number and your existing chats; we connect it in about 15 minutes, or give you a fresh business number.

What if the AI doesn’t know an answer?+

It says so honestly and offers to connect the customer to you — or takes a message. You can also teach Pally new answers anytime from the dashboard.

Can I still reply myself?+

Anytime. Send “Pally, pause” from your phone and you take over instantly; send “resume” and it’s back on duty. You can also jump into any live chat with one tap.

Is my customers’ data safe?+

Messages are encrypted in transit and at rest, never used to train shared models, and you can export or delete everything with one click.

I’m not tech-savvy. How hard is setup?+

If you can send a WhatsApp message, you can set up Pally. Upload your price list or menu, connect your calendar, and go. Most owners are live the same day.
